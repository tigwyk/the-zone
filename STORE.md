## Description

You are a stalker walking into the Zone — a quarantined wasteland where physics
has gone soft, artifacts are worth more than your life, and rumour says the centre
grants your wish.

Thirty hand-authored areas, drawn in ASCII and driven by a menu of what makes
sense right here. Seven things that fight back. Twelve artifacts. Four vendors,
ten people to talk to, twenty jobs, six endings.

**The art is the game.** Hidden exits are drawn into the scenery as bold cyan
letters. Ten of the thirty areas have no menu entry at all — you reach them by
noticing something in the picture.

**Combat** is turn-based on action points. Guns take a caliber and hold a
magazine, so a sawn-off spends a turn in three reloading and a rifle barely
notices. Ammunition moves your accuracy, your damage and how much armour you
punch through.

**The Zone does not wait.** Radiation drags your attributes down and kills at a
thousand. Every few days an emission burns the sky and you get an hour of warning
to find cover.

Death deletes the save. What carries to the next stalker is the lore you found, a
quarter of your faction standing, and a memorial entry with your name and how you
died.

Skills rise by use. There is no XP table. Arrow keys and Enter drive the menus;
letter keys belong to the secrets.
